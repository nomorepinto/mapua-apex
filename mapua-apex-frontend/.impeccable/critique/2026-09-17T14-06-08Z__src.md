#### Overall Impression

The product already looks like Mapúa from the left edge of the screen. Everything to the right still looks like a competent internal tool: even weight, even radius, even grey type. The single biggest visual opportunity is to **move brand and hierarchy into the work** — one primary action in maroon, one focal region per page, and type that can be read without squinting — instead of decorating a generic dashboard.

#### What's Working

1. **Sidebar identity.** Skyline mark + Audiowide **APEX** + gold expansion on `#8B0000` is immediately campus-correct, including the mobile sheet.
2. **Create start + Guide.** `/students/submissions` is a real single-focus gate. The Guide modal (maroon/gold step cards) is the most product-specific teaching surface in the app.
3. **Signatory consequence copy.** Return/Reject dialogs explain desk metaphor and finality before the officer types — rare and right for this domain.

#### Priority Issues

**[P1] The SAAF is still one paper dump**
- **What:** Classification, ~12 proponent fields, activity details, mission checkboxes, SDGs, and a budget grid share one scrolling sheet with no stepper and no restated event title.
- **Why it matters:** First-time org officers cannot hold that much at once. The visual is a wall, not a desk. It is the least pleasing and least usable surface in the product.
- **Fix:** 3–4 disclosed steps (Classification → People → Activity → Alignment & budget) with a persistent progress rail and the event title always visible. Drop full-uppercase section shouts.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill` (then `/impeccable layout` on the stepper)

**[P1] Review actions invert the visual decision**
- **What:** In `activity-detail-modal.tsx`, **Return Proposal** is solid `bg-neutral-900`; **Approve Proposal** is a white outline ghost. **Reject** already uses maroon. Approve has no confirm. Dummy chips “REVIEWING TARGET” / “TIME SUBMITTED” occupy the header with no data.
- **Why it matters:** The loudest yes in the product is visually the quietest. Officers will return by default or approve by accident. Maroon is used to destroy, not to endorse.
- **Fix:** Approve = maroon (or maroon+gold) primary with confirm (“Endorse General Assembly 2026?”). Return = secondary. Reject stays destructive. Fill the chips with the real date and current signatory.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] The org home is not the work, and the canvas is visually flat**
- **What:** Org dashboard leads with Announcements. **Create Project/Event** fights **Submissions** (which is create again). Resource Quick Links look clickable and only alert “coming soon.” Empty states and table headers use `#94A3B8` at 11px–xs. The live detector flagged seven low-contrast hits and three tiny-text hits here.
- **Why it matters:** The first screen after login does not look like a place to file or track an event. Slate-on-grey empties make the page feel unfinished, not calm.
- **Fix:** Lead with the submission table and a single create CTA. Compact announcements. Remove or wire resource links. Raise empty/meta type to `text-sm` and `neutral-500`+ (4.5:1). Rename nav: Dashboard (track) vs New proposal (create).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Admin maintenance looks unfinished**
- **What:** Org/signatory edit is right-click only. UUIDs are shown as badges. **Admin System Settings** claims configuration and shows three hardcoded lines. Document title is still `vite-app`; favicon is still `vite.svg`.
- **Why it matters:** The chrome says institution; the tab and settings say prototype. Keyboard, touch, and first-timers cannot edit rows.
- **Fix:** Visible row action (Edit). Hide UUIDs. Ship Settings or remove it. Title: `APEX · Mapúa`. Swap the Vite favicon.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Task nav includes credits, a 9.6px tagline, and a dangerous Sign Out**
- **What:** **About the Devs** is a first-class destination (1 of 2 for signatories). Joke titles undercut OSA seriousness. Sidebar tagline is `text-[0.6rem]` gold (detector: 9.6px body text). Sign Out sits with destinations, no confirm.
- **Why it matters:** The most distinctive visual in the product — the rail — is also the noisiest and the hardest to read. Credits in the task IA cheapen the desk.
- **Fix:** Move About under the user chip. Confirm sign-out. Raise the expansion line to ~12px and let it live on one line, or drop it from the rail and keep it on About.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`
